# Product and Delivery resources

## Must read

- *Inspired*, Marty Cagan - the chapters on product risk and on talking to users. The
  risk taxonomy is the part that maps onto AI features, where the risk is that you
  build the wrong thing rather than that you build it badly.
  https://www.svpg.com/inspired/
- *Shape Up*, Ryan Singer - the fixed-cycle approach to scoping a bet, and the section on
  shaping the work before it is scheduled.
  https://basecamp.com/shapeup
- *The Mom Test*, Rob Fitzpatrick - the rules for talking to users without leading them
  to the answer you want.
- *Good Strategy / Bad Strategy*, Richard Rumelt - the core distinction between a goal
  with a deadline and a strategy with a diagnosis, a guiding policy and coherent action.
- *Continuous Discovery*, Teresa Torres - one chapter on opportunity solution trees is
  enough for this module.

## Watch

- The AI product talks from industry conferences - watch for the ones that talk about
  rollout and failure, not the ones about capabilities.
- The postmortem templates used by public incident write-ups - read three good ones and
  note the structure they share; it is the structure the failure message draft borrows.
- "Continuous Discovery" podcast episodes on interviewing users - useful if the module's
  "talk to users first" step is the one you are least practised at.

## Skip

- Pricing theory beyond unit economics - you need margin per segment, not a pricing
  course.
- Growth loops, referral mechanics and viral design - the module is about shipping one
  feature well.
- Brand, copy and launch campaigns - the failure message is the only piece of writing
  this module asks for.
